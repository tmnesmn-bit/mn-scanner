Fire, EMS, and most suburban and outstate agencies below are still in the clear — Minneapolis Fire, for example, streams live in Hennepin County.

Know before you listen

In a vehicle: Minnesota Statute 299C.37 makes it a misdemeanor to equip a motor vehicle with a radio receiver capable of picking up police transmissions without a permit from the Bureau of Criminal Apprehension. Whether that applies to a phone app has not been settled by Minnesota courts — listening at home is legal.

Never use scanner audio to interfere with a scene, respond to calls, or aid a crime — using intercepted communications in furtherance of a crime is its own offense.
